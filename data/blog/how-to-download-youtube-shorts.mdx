YouTube Shorts are just regular YouTube videos with a `/shorts/` URL and a vertical aspect ratio, but a couple of practical differences change how downloading them plays out.

## Steps

1. Open the Short and copy its URL — either from the address bar or the Share button, which gives you a link containing `/shorts/`.
2. Paste it into a [YouTube video downloader](/youtube-video-downloader).
3. Pick a quality and download.

The tool doesn't need a separate "Shorts mode" — a Short's URL is recognized the same way any YouTube video URL is.

## What's Different About Shorts

**Resolution ceiling.** Shorts are usually uploaded at 1080x1920 (vertical) rather than the 1920x1080 landscape most regular videos use, so the quality options you'll see reflect that — don't expect a 4K option on a Short even if 4K exists for other content from the same channel.

**Shorter length, faster processing.** Since Shorts are capped at a few minutes by YouTube itself, downloads are typically much faster than for a full-length video — there's no merge-and-wait delay for a 45-minute file.

**Audio behaves the same as any other video.** If the Short has music or a voiceover, it downloads with the video the same way a regular video's audio does — no separate step needed.

## Common Issues

**"This video isn't available."** Same causes as any YouTube video — the Short is private, age-restricted, or was deleted. A tool has no way around this since it only accesses what YouTube makes public.

**Downloaded video plays sideways or cropped oddly.** This usually means the tool grabbed a transcoded/re-encoded version rather than the source file — worth trying a different quality option if this happens.

## FAQ

**Do I need a different tool for Shorts vs. regular YouTube videos?**
No — the same downloader handles both, since a Short is just a YouTube video at a specific URL pattern.

**Can I download just the audio from a Short?**
Yes, if the tool offers an audio-only option, the same way it would for a full-length video.

**Is there a length limit on Shorts downloads?**
Shorts are already short by YouTube's own rules, so they're well under any length limit a downloader would enforce.
