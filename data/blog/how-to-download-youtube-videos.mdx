YouTube doesn't give you a native download button on the web (outside of YouTube Premium's offline mode, which only works inside the app). Here's the practical way to save a public video to your device.

## The Basic Steps

1. Copy the video's URL from the address bar, or tap Share → Copy Link on mobile.
2. Paste it into a [YouTube video downloader](/youtube-video-downloader).
3. Wait for the preview — title, channel, duration and thumbnail should all load.
4. Pick a quality (or Audio for MP3) and download.

## Picking a Quality

Downloaders that show real quality buttons only list resolutions that actually exist for that specific video — a short vertical clip might only have up to 720p available, while a full production upload could go up to 1080p or higher. If a tool shows the same fixed list (360p / 720p / 1080p) on every single video regardless of the source, some of those options are likely to fail.

## Video vs. Audio-Only

If you only need the sound — a podcast segment, a lecture, background music — downloading the full video file first is wasted bandwidth. A dedicated [Audio (MP3) option](/youtube-to-mp3) skips the video stream entirely and gives you a much smaller file.

## What Makes a Downloader Reliable

The difference between a smooth download and a frustrating one usually comes down to three things: whether the preview is real (not a generic placeholder), whether the download button goes straight to a file instead of an ad redirect, and whether the listed quality options are accurate. ## Common Issues

**"This video isn't available" or similar error.** Usually means the video is private, age-restricted, or region-locked — none of which a browser-based tool can bypass, since it only has access to what YouTube makes publicly available.

**Download stuck or very slow.** Longer videos in higher resolutions take longer to process server-side before the file is ready — this is normal for videos approaching the length limit most tools enforce (commonly around 1 hour).

## FAQ

**Is downloading YouTube videos legal?**
Saving public videos for personal use is generally fine; redistributing or reuploading copyrighted content is not.

**Do I need software or a browser extension?**
No — a web-based downloader works from any device without installing anything.

**Can I download YouTube Shorts?**
Yes, as long as the Short is public.
