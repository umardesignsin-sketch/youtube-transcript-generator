Pinterest doesn't make it easy to save what you find. There's no built-in "download" button on most pins, right-clicking an image often only grabs a low-res thumbnail, and videos can't be saved at all through the normal interface. Here's what actually works.

## Why Pinterest Doesn't Have a Download Button

Pinterest is built around driving traffic back to the original source (a blog, a shop, a portfolio), not around being a file host. That's a deliberate product decision, not an oversight — which is why saving media from it usually requires a separate tool rather than a built-in feature.

## Downloading a Pinterest Video

1. Open the pin and copy its link (tap Share → Copy Link, or copy the URL from your browser's address bar).
2. Paste it into a [Pinterest downloader](/pinterest-downloader).
3. The tool detects it's a video pin and gives you a direct download link, plus an MP3-audio-only option.

This works for both standard video pins and **Idea Pins** (Pinterest's multi-page story format) — the two store video data differently behind the scenes, so a downloader needs to handle both, not just one.

## Downloading a Pinterest Image

Same first two steps — paste the pin link into the [Pinterest Image Downloader](/pinterest-image-downloader) — but you get the original full-resolution photo back, not the scaled-down thumbnail you'd get from a browser right-click.

## Why Right-Click "Save Image" Often Disappoints

The image displayed on a Pinterest board is frequently a resized preview, not the source file. A right-click save grabs whatever's actually loaded in the browser at that moment — which is the compressed preview version, not the original upload. Going through the pin's own data (rather than the rendered page) is what gets you the real file.

## Common Issues

**"This pin isn't found" or similar error.** Usually means the pin was deleted, or the link was copied incorrectly (make sure it's the full `pinterest.com/pin/...` URL).

**Private boards.** Only pins on public boards can be downloaded — there's no way around this, since the tool only has access to what Pinterest itself makes publicly available.

**Idea Pins with multiple pages.** Each page of an Idea Pin can contain its own video or image; a downloader needs to walk through the pin's full data structure to catch content beyond the first page.

## FAQ

**Is downloading from Pinterest legal?**
Saving content for personal use is generally fine, but the underlying image or video may still be copyrighted by its original creator — don't republish or resell it without permission.

**Do I need the Pinterest app or a browser extension?**
No, a web-based downloader works from any device without installing anything.

**Can I download an entire board at once?**
Not with a single-pin tool like this — you'd paste each pin's link individually.
