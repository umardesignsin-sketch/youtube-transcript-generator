Every public YouTube video that has captions — whether added by the creator or generated automatically using [YouTube's speech recognition](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6373554) — has a transcript hiding behind it. You don't need to install anything to get it out. Here's exactly how.

## Method 1: Use a Free Online Transcript Generator

The fastest way is a dedicated web tool built for this one job.

1. Copy the URL of the YouTube video (from the address bar, or the Share button).
2. Paste it into our [free YouTube Transcript Generator](/youtube-transcript-generator).
3. Click **Generate Transcript**. The tool detects available caption languages and pulls the text in seconds.
4. Copy the transcript, or download it as a plain TXT file, a timestamped TXT file, or an SRT subtitle file.

No account, no browser extension, and the video doesn't need to be yours — it just needs to be public and have captions available.

## Method 2: YouTube's Built-In Transcript Panel

YouTube also exposes transcripts natively, though it's more manual:

1. Open the video on YouTube (desktop web).
2. Click the **...** (more) button below the video.
3. Select **Show transcript**.
4. The transcript appears in a panel next to the video, with timestamps for each line.

This works well if you just want to read along, but copying it out cleanly (without timestamps mixed into every line) and downloading it as a file takes extra manual work.

## Which Method Should You Use?

If you want clean text with timestamps you can actually download in a usable format, a dedicated generator is faster. YouTube's built-in panel is fine for reading along in the moment but isn't built for exporting.

## Common Reasons People Need a Transcript

- **Studying** — searching a lecture transcript for a concept is faster than scrubbing through video.
- **Content repurposing** — turning a video into a blog post, thread or newsletter.
- **Research** — citing interviews, talks or panels with an accurate, searchable source.
- **AI workflows** — pasting a transcript into ChatGPT or Claude to summarize, extract key points, or generate notes.
- **Accessibility** — providing a text alternative to video content, [an established web accessibility practice](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/).
